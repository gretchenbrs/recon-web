Business Requirement: Order-Payment Reconciliation

Objective:
Reconcile order records from the order system with payment records from the gateway.

Rules:
1. Match orders.order_id with payments.txn_reference
2. Payment must occur within 2 days after order_date
3. Only include payments with status = completed or settled
4. Flag amount mismatches where abs(order_amount - paid_amount) > 2 USD
5. Identify:
   - Missing payments
   - Orphan payments
   - Amount mismatches
   - Late payments

Metrics:
- Match rate
- Missing payment count
- Orphan payment count
- Total mismatch value
- Avg payment delay
